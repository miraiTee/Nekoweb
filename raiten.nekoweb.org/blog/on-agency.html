Thinking why I named myself Mirai instead of a different name. Maybe I was just really stupid and did the most boring tgirl job ever of converting your first name from masc to fem >.>) Or maybe I was a tad bit orientalist and thought "Mirai" was a really good name. Well this name's a bit good for my cadence anyway and it's cute and snappy.

Why a detour on my nature, well it's the duality of Mirai and Shourai, or the far future and near future. Well, I doubt I'd really live that future. It's still very illegal to be trans in my damn counry (Philippines) where my existence is literally just "under their watchful gaze" because i pass. (unless my legal name gets shafted in which case LOL back to transphobia.)

Something that goes back is to recall what is at the very least recall my childhood. Much of it is hazy nowadays because nobody really cared about me at all. and nobody is really left at recording what really happened. Even I myself wasn't a good blogger, note taker or a writer at a time. Neither my mother or father really knew what to do with me except at the very symbolic meaning of a "child" per se. My mother wanted me to be an achiever, but she never really cared outside of scrubbing me off the moment I try to atleast learn or impress. Neither was my father who I can feel the "false bravado" he tries to put up impressing me. They kept lying to my face they knew what I was, but do they know what they were? Or did they wash their hands clean now.

I was never... cared... for.. the very slightest. I was fend off to the wolves and only the internet was my sanctuary. WIthout any parental guidance I'm like injected with the most colorful strands of time and tapestry and whatever... so I finally hit adulthood feeling like an utterly brainbroken and developmentally stunted pile of shit. Yeah the trans part is like 1/10th of the car crash that is my childhood. I do not know the customs nor the norms of whatever place I know. I can read yes, but the way "normal people" easily intuit is someting I could and would never attain ever again. This does mean I have the capacity to understand and piece everything as if I was a child, and the capacity to wield the knowledge as if I was an adult. Yet I don't feel like fully either or.

To even think about the time spent, not in a goddamn closet, but a time safe, one that would basically glitch you out of existence in and out. both past and future, blur the line of childhood and adulthood. An arbitrary suffering where I was hoping a single person would ever bust me out. A single person out of the milleu that I signal in everyday life, but no one came. Not even a single iota of soul who has the power. A loop locked from the outside, where the only way forward is to break it from the inside.

An arbitrary suffering out from their very hands blast it open apart and... they don't even recognize it. They don't even understand what hell I came out of from. You have the audacity to shame me now after being non-existent and locked and key in that singular space. I shrinked out of fear of the Pandora's box that I already am, but there was no remorse, not even an acknowledgement out from their very eyes. And now they come for me... trying to convince themselves the hardest that they were there at the very juncion of my childhood they oh so abandoned. But they can't really name what I faced, because only I knew the keys and lock in there. And they will never even dare try to know it for how much of a cogitohazard it all is.

They cannot accept the very... no... I cannot accept the pain I had was unneccessary in the first place. To even begin thinking the hellzone was the only way to live now. The only thing keeping me sane is my delusions really. But I had to bite first the bullet nothing really wants to come for me and have to carve and make peace with my suffering.

"In the same way, a man whose limbs had been bound from birth, but who had neverless found out how to hobble about, might attribute to the very bands that bound him his ability to move, while, on the contrary, they would diminish and paralyze the muscular energy of his limbs.," said Malatasta on Anarchy. Maybe all this pain was worth it to make a person out of me. My parents never raised me, the internets gets every single credit for making or unmaking the person who I am.
